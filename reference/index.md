## Synchrosqueezing, chirplets and de-chirped spectra

Sharpened scalograms, chirp-matched spectra and checks of an age model
in the de-chirped record.

- [`analyse_wavelet_sst()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_wavelet_sst.md)
  [`analyze_wavelet_sst()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_wavelet_sst.md)
  : Wavelet synchrosqueezing transform
- [`analyse_superlet_sst()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_superlet_sst.md)
  [`analyze_superlet_sst()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_superlet_sst.md)
  : Superlet synchrosqueezing: consensus of synchrosqueezed transforms
  with short and long wavelets
- [`analyse_chirplet_sst()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_chirplet_sst.md)
  [`analyze_chirplet_sst()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_chirplet_sst.md)
  : Synchrosqueezed wavelet-chirplet transform
- [`analyse_conceft()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_conceft.md)
  [`analyze_conceft()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_conceft.md)
  : Multitaper synchrosqueezing (ConceFT) of a record
- [`analyse_chirp_wavelet()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_chirp_wavelet.md)
  [`analyze_chirp_wavelet()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_chirp_wavelet.md)
  : Chirp-matched wavelet scalogram with the astronomical template over
  rate and rate gradient
- [`analyse_chirp_eha()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_chirp_eha.md)
  [`analyze_chirp_eha()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_chirp_eha.md)
  : Chirp-matched evolutive harmonic analysis (de-chirped windowed
  multitaper spectra)
- [`chirp_eha_significance()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/chirp_eha_significance.md)
  : Significance of the lines of a (de-chirped) EHA map against warped
  noise
- [`plot_chirp_eha()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/plot_chirp_eha.md)
  : Plot a chirp-matched EHA map, with or without the surrogate
  significance
- [`rate_gram()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/rate_gram.md)
  : Rate-gram: the astronomical template as a spectrum over
  sedimentation rate, per window
- [`analyse_dechirped()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/analyse_dechirped.md)
  : De-chirped scalogram of any transform: the record warped to time
  with a track, transformed, mapped back to depth
- [`plot_dechirped()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/plot_dechirped.md)
  : Plot a de-chirped scalogram
- [`check_agemodel()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/check_agemodel.md)
  : Check an age model against the de-chirped record: where do the
  astronomical cycles bend away?
- [`plot_agemodel_check()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/plot_agemodel_check.md)
  : Plot an age-model check: the de-chirped maps, the rate corrections
  the cycles ask for and the flagged intervals
- [`detect_transients()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/detect_transients.md)
  : Transient (impulsive) features of a record by transient extraction

## Significance and uncertainty of spectra

- [`wavelet_significance()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wavelet_significance.md)
  : Significance of wavelet and superlet power: analytic and surrogate
  (Monte Carlo) levels
- [`wavelet_band_significance()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wavelet_band_significance.md)
  [`plot(`*`<wr_band_significance>`*`)`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wavelet_band_significance.md)
  : Significance of scale-averaged wavelet power (Torrence and Compo,
  section 4d)
- [`sst_significance()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/sst_significance.md)
  : Significance of synchrosqueezed (and other) scalograms against
  autocorrelated noise
- [`model_red_noise_wt()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/model_red_noise_wt.md)
  : Models average spectral power based curves based on a red-noise
  signal generated using the characteristics of an input signal.
- [`percentile_from_red_noise()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/percentile_from_red_noise.md)
  : Calculate average spectral power from red noise curves for a given
  percentile
- [`wavelet_uncertainty()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wavelet_uncertainty.md)
  : Calculate the uncertainty associated with the wavelet analysis based
  on the Gabor uncertainty principle
- [`fft_uncertainty()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/fft_uncertainty.md)
  : Calculate the uncertainty associated with a tracked cycle based on
  the frequency uncertainty of the FFT
- [`fft_frequency_uncertainty()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/fft_frequency_uncertainty.md)
  : Frequency uncertainty of the Fourier transform (FFT) for a record
  length
- [`wr_coloured_noise()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wr_coloured_noise.md)
  [`wr_brewer_palettes()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wr_coloured_noise.md)
  [`wr_colored_noise()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/wr_coloured_noise.md)
  : Colour noise and palette helpers

## Tracking cycles

Interactive and automatic tracking of the period of a cycle and of the
sedimentation rate.

- [`track_period()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_period.md)
  : Track the period of a cycle in a wavelet or superlet scalogram
- [`track_period_wavelet()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_period_wavelet.md)
  : Track the period of a cycle in a wavelet spectra
- [`delpts_tracked_period_wt()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/delpts_tracked_period_wt.md)
  : Remove tracking points which were tracked in a wavelet spectra
- [`completed_series()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/completed_series.md)
  : Complete the tracking of the period a cycle in a scalogram
- [`retrack_wt_MC()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/retrack_wt_MC.md)
  : Re-track cycles using a Monte-Carlo simulation
- [`loess_auto()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/loess_auto.md)
  : Perform an automatically loess based smoothing of a time series
- [`track_ridge_hmm()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_ridge_hmm.md)
  : Continuous tracking of a cycle in a (synchrosqueezed) scalogram with
  a hidden Markov model
- [`track_astro_hmm()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_astro_hmm.md)
  : Astronomically constrained continuous tracking of the sedimentation
  rate in a scalogram
- [`track_astro_auto()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_astro_auto.md)
  : Recommended workflow for continuous astronomical tracking of noisy
  records
- [`track_astro_iterative()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_astro_iterative.md)
  : Iterative (demodulated) astronomical tracking of the sedimentation
  rate
- [`track_chirp_hmm()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/track_chirp_hmm.md)
  : Chirp-steered tracking of the sedimentation rate (rate and rate
  gradient as the hidden state)
- [`plot_track()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/plot_track.md)
  : Plot a continuous track on its scalogram
- [`plot_astro_evidence()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/plot_astro_evidence.md)
  : Map of the astronomical evidence against sedimentation rate and
  depth
- [`astro_periods_tracking()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/astro_periods_tracking.md)
  : Astronomical periods and weights for tracking, for any age
- [`as_wr_track()`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/as_wr_track.md)
  : Build a track (depth, sedimentation rate, time) from a tracked
  period curve, a rate curve or an age model
