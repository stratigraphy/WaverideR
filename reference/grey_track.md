# Tracking points of the precession (22 kyr cycle) IODP 926 grey scale (154-174m) record of Zeeden et al., (2013)

Example data which consists of tracking points of the precession (22 kyr
cycle) in the wavelet scalogram of the IODP 926 grey scale (154-174m)
record of Zeeden et al., (2013)

## Details

Column 1: Depth (meters)  
Column 2: period (meters)
