# Earth's polar moment of inertia at spin rate omega (Farhat et al. 2022, A.3)

Earth's polar moment of inertia at spin rate omega (Farhat et al. 2022,
A.3)

## Usage

``` r
earth_moment_of_inertia(omega)
```

## Arguments

- omega:

  Angular velocity (spin rate) of the Earth, in rad/s (numeric, scalar
  or vector, \> 0), e.g. from
  [`earth_omega`](https://stratigraphy.github.io/WaverideR/reference/earth_omega.md).
  Present-day value is about 7.292e-5 rad/s. Required (no default).
